Journalists covering iGaming, fintech or crypto arrive at a pitch with
more scepticism than they would for a consumer SaaS product, and
reasonably so — these sectors have earned their share of scrutiny. A
generic press release does not survive contact with that scepticism.

## Lead with data, not announcements

A product launch announcement rarely earns coverage in a regulated
sector; a data study usually does. Usage statistics, anonymised customer
behaviour trends, or original survey data give a journalist something
to write about that isn't simply repeating your marketing.

## Address compliance before they have to ask

Pitches that pre-empt the obvious compliance question — how is this
regulated, what licence applies, what happened last time this topic was
covered — get further than pitches that hope nobody asks. Journalists in
these beats have heard enough vague reassurance to be allergic to it.

## Build relationships, not one-off pitches

The outlets that cover iGaming, fintech and crypto seriously are a
smaller list than general business press, and reporters on that beat
move between publications. A single good pitch treated as transactional
wastes a relationship that would have been worth cultivating over
several stories.
