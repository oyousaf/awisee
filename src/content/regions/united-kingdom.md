## Link building in the UK

The UK media landscape rewards precision over volume — a placement in the
wrong trade publication does little, while the right one carries weight for
months. Our approach favours a smaller number of well-matched placements
over a high-volume, low-relevance campaign.

## What this looks like in practice

Campaigns typically combine editorial link placements with digital PR
angles suited to UK journalists' appetite for original data and clear
news hooks, coordinated with your existing content calendar rather than
run as a separate workstream.
