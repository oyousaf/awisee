## Link building in Brazil

Brazil's iGaming and fintech sectors move quickly, and its publishers
reflect that pace — content cycles are shorter and outreach windows
close faster than in more conservative European markets. Our
Brazil-based team runs on the market's own rhythm rather than importing
a slower international process.

## What this looks like in practice

Given Brazil's evolving regulatory environment for gambling advertising
specifically, placements are checked against current rules before
publication, not just at the strategy stage.
