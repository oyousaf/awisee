## Link building in Germany

German publishers are typically more conservative about sponsored or
promotional content than their counterparts elsewhere in Europe, and
readers are quick to notice when a placement reads as translated rather
than written for them. We work with in-market writers for this reason,
not a single template adapted across languages.

## What this looks like in practice

Expect a slower, more deliberate outreach cadence than in less
regulation-conscious markets — fewer placements, each with a higher bar
for relevance and disclosure compliance.
